argument-hint: "[meeting name, person, or company; default: next meeting with an outside attendee]"

Prepare me for a meeting.

Target: $ARGUMENTS. If empty, use my next calendar event today or tomorrow that has at least one attendee outside my own email domain.

Research, read-only:
1. **The event**: time, duration, location or link, the attendees, and the description or agenda.
2. **Our history**: search Gmail for past threads with each attendee and their company. Summarize what was said, what was promised, and any open threads.
3. **Who they are**: for each outside attendee, their role, team, and one or two notable things (recent posts, projects, talks) from public web search. For companies: what they do, recent news in the last 90 days, and the product or team relevant to me.
4. **My angle**: if this is an interview or recruiting chat, pull the matching job description (from email or the web) and map the 3 requirements that matter most to my strongest matching experience. Check local notes or resume files if they exist.

Output one page:
- **In one line:** why this meeting exists and what a great outcome looks like.
- **People:** a two-line bio each, plus "last talked" if there's history.
- **Talking points:** 3 bullets.
- **Questions to ask:** 3 smart ones, specific to them, not generic.
- **Watch-outs:** anything sensitive, overdue, or awkward.
- **Logistics:** time in my timezone, link or room, and what to have open.

Ask before saving the brief anywhere. Offer to save it as a Notion page or attach it to the event description.
